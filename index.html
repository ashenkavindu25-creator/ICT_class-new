Meet Ashen Disanayaka

I teach ICT so that every student understands how technology works, not just how to memorise answers. My classes mix clear theory, hands-on practice and past-paper training.

From a Grade 6 student opening a computer for the first time to a Grade 11 student preparing for the O/L, each class is built around the student's level.

  • Simple explanations in Sinhala or English
  • Practical lessons and weekly practice
  • O/L past papers and model paper discussions
  • Small groups so every student gets attention
